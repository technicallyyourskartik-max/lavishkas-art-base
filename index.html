Good to know

Do I need any experience?

None at all. We show you each step, and children and adults start from the same place.

What ages can join?

Canvas painting starts at 6, with a grown-up staying for children under 10. Watercolour Studio is for ages 12 and up.

What should I wear?

Clothes you don’t mind getting marked. We provide an apron, but paint travels.

When do I get my piece?

Both pieces go home with you at the end of the session.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

You can move your booking once if you tell us 24 hours before. Inside 24 hours, the advance is not refundable.

How does payment work?

Pay a small advance per person on UPI to hold your seat, and the balance at the studio. You can also pay in full up front. Your seat is confirmed once we reply on WhatsApp.
