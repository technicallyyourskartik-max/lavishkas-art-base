Good to know

Do I need any experience?

None at all. We show you each step, and kids and grown-ups start at the same place.

What age can join?

Most workshops start at 6, with a grown-up for under 10s. The pottery wheel is best from 10 and up.

What should I wear?

Clothes that can get messy. We give you an apron, but clay and paint have minds of their own.

When do I get my piece?

Canvas, resin and moon lamps are ready the same day or the next. Plates and clay go through the kiln, so pick them up in about ten days. We message you when they’re ready.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

You can move your booking once if you tell us 24 hours before. Inside 24 hours, the advance isn’t refundable.

How does payment work?

Pay a small advance per person on UPI to hold your seat, and the rest at the studio. You can also pay in full up front. Your seat is confirmed once we reply on WhatsApp.
