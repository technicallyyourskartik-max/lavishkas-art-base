How booking works

  1. Pick a session on the timetable.

    You can book up to 8 seats in one go.

  2. Pay the full amount on UPI.

    Scan the QR code or tap to pay from any UPI app. Your booking reference is filled in for you.

  3. Send us the payment screenshot on WhatsApp.

    Your seat is confirmed once we reply.

  4. Arrive ten minutes early.

    We take care of the rest. Everything is already paid for.

Bringing a group?

Birthdays, team outings and school groups of six or more can ask for a private batch. Tell us the date and the number of people, and we will plan the session around you.

Contact the studio

Good to know

Do I need any experience?

None at all. We show you each step, and children and adults start from the same place.

What ages can join?

Each workshop lists its minimum age, from 6 up to 12. A grown-up stays with children under 10.

What should I wear?

Clothes you don’t mind getting marked. We provide an apron, but paint, ink and wax travel.

When do I get my piece?

Most pieces go home with you at the end of the session. If something needs time to set, such as a mosaic or a candle, we will tell you how long during the session.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

You can move your booking once if you tell us 24 hours before. Inside 24 hours, the payment is not refundable.

How does payment work?

You pay the full amount up front on UPI, then send us the screenshot on WhatsApp. Your seat is confirmed once we reply, and there is nothing more to pay at the studio.
